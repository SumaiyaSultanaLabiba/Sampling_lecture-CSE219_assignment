# Sampling_lecture-CSE219_assignment

📚 Continuous-Time Signal Sampling, Aliasing & Reconstruction | CSE 219

Welcome! This repository covers the fundamental concepts of continuous-time signal sampling, the Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem, practical reconstruction techniques, and the Fourier duality connecting the DFT and DTFT.This lecture explores both the mathematical derivations and real-world intuition behind signal sampling and reconstruction.

📄 Companion Handout & PDF

Follow the video tutorial along with the complete PDF document created for this lecture:🔗 GitHub Repository: Sampling.pdf Lecture Notes

💡 Topics Covered

Introduction to Sampling & Impulse Multiplication

Derivation of the Sampled Frequency Spectrum $X_p(j\omega)$

Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem & Aliasing

Signal Reconstruction (Ideal LPF & Sinc Interpolation)

Practical Reconstruction: Zero-Order Hold (ZOH) & First-Order Hold (FOH)

Inevitability of Aliasing in Real-World Signals

Connection of Sampling with DTFT and DFT (Frequency Sampling Duality)
